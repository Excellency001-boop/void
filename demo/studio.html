VOIDvoice studio. Read along, record, done.
Before you start
  1. Use headphones. If you use speakers the video's room sound is not a problem (it is silent), but headphones keep your recording clean.
  2. Quiet room, phone on silent, mouth about a hand span from the mic.
  3. Rehearse first. The teleprompter shows each line a few seconds early. Read it like you are showing a friend.
  4. Mistakes are fine. Just press Record again. A take is 2 minutes.
