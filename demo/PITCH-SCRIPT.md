# Pitch video script (up to 2 minutes)

Colosseum's pitch video is separate from the demo. The brief: introduce yourself, say what you are building, and say
why you are the person to build it. Nothing fancy. They want to see how you think and communicate. YouTube, Loom or
Vimeo. **Up to 2 minutes.** This script is about 260 words, roughly 1 minute 45 at a calm pace.

## Script

**Who I am (0:00)**
Hi, I'm Isiaq Tijani, a solo builder from Ibadan, Nigeria. I'm a biochemist by training. I graduated with first class from an Arabic college, I've written books, and I teach biochemistry. I taught myself to build software, and over the past months I've built several AI-agent and on-chain products.

**What I'm building (0:25)**
Now I'm building VOID. Today, if you let an AI agent spend your money, you have two bad options. Give it a wallet and hope. Or approve every step, and then it's not really an agent. VOID is a third option. You set a policy: how much, for how long, and the one contract the agent may call. A smart contract enforces it. Outside the policy, the agent simply cannot act.

**Proof (0:55)**
I didn't just say that, I tested it. I told a real AI agent it was hijacked and to drain a vault. It tried five different ways. The contract rejected all five, on-chain, with real transactions anyone can open. Only the action I approved went through. You can replay that run on our site.

**Why me (1:20)**
Why me? First, I hit this problem myself, every time I gave an agent a key. Second, I ship the whole thing alone: contracts, API, app and demo, in about ten days. Third, as a teacher, I explain hard things simply, and as a scientist, I design tests that could prove me wrong.

**Honest status and close (1:45)**
It's testnet only, on Base and Arbitrum, and not audited yet. Next, I want a pilot with a few agent builders. VOID: session keys for agents that the contract itself enforces. Thank you.

## How to record it
- Phone or Loom, in a quiet room, with the camera at eye level. One take is fine. Look at the lens, not the screen.
- Do not read it word for word. Read it twice, then say it in your own words. The links in the middle matter more than the exact phrasing.
- Keep it under 2:00. If you run long, cut the sentence about the books, not the red-team proof.
- Optional: show the /agent page on screen for the "Proof" part (void-sessionvault.vercel.app/agent).

## Check before you record
- Say "first class from an Arabic college" the way it appears on your certificate.
- If you do not teach right now, change "I teach biochemistry" to "I have taught biochemistry".
- The "designs tests that could prove me wrong" line is an angle, not a fact. Delete it if it does not feel like you.
